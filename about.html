Our story

From a trusted showroom to a modern food brand.

SK TABII is a new chapter for a family-led dates and nuts business — bringing premium natural foods into a refined, accessible brand.

The idea

Built around nature, quality and trust.

SK TABII is designed to grow beyond dates and nuts into a broader natural foods destination, including dry fruits, organic pantry products, honey, seeds and curated gifting.

The identity pairs deep botanical green with warm gold to reflect the natural origin and premium character of the range.
